# Public experiments

These experiments are the demonstrated Video Intelligence capability within Applied Intelligence. They are technical evidence and experimental infrastructure, not industrial benchmarks, commercial case studies, or guarantees of performance in a client environment.

## Flow Intelligence

A short public traffic proxy was processed with YOLO and ByteTrack. The demonstration generated **62 processed frames**, **55 unique tracks** and **2,377 event rows**. The resulting events are a demonstration of transforming visual observations into structured operational signals.

## Semantic Video Search

Detected-object crops were indexed with CLIP and queried through Qdrant. The demonstration indexed **499 objects** and achieved **100% Recall@10 on 3 diagnostic queries**. This is a small diagnostic on proxy footage, not a benchmark or a guarantee of domain performance.

## Interpretation

Flow Intelligence and Semantic Search are presented as capabilities in operation. They show that mechanisms for detection, tracking, events, metrics, embeddings, semantic retrieval and evaluation can be combined into an experimental pipeline. They do not claim that a finished product exists for a particular customer problem.
