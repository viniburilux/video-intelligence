# Limitations

Public proxy footage does not represent a client’s cameras, lighting, density, procedures or legal context. Tracking is not identity. Object-crop retrieval is not full scene understanding. Metrics are illustrative and should be revalidated on authorized, representative data.

The public experiments are evidence of technical capability and experimental infrastructure. They are not industrial benchmarks, claims about a client environment, or proof that a mechanism will transfer without new investigation.

No client name, commercial opportunity registry, sensitive hypothesis, personal data or third-party dataset is included in this public repository.

Applied Intelligence may investigate additional modalities and operational contexts, but this repository must not be read as claiming that every listed modality or application has already been implemented or commercially validated.
