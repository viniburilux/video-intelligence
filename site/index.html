Applied Intelligence

We turn operational data into actionable intelligence.

We are an applied engineering and R&D team that investigates, experiments, and builds intelligence systems for problems that do not yet have a ready-made solution.

You do not need to know which AI you need. You need to know which problem you need to solve.
We investigate the technological path.
Problem→Hypothesis→Experiment→Evidence→Decision→System

O problema vem antes da tecnologia

Problemas reais não chegam com o modelo, framework ou algoritmo escolhido. Eles chegam como problema, contexto operacional e dados disponíveis. A tecnologia é escolhida em função do problema — não o contrário.

Experimentação não é o fim. O objetivo é produzir evidência suficiente para decidir o que vale desenvolver, o que precisa ser reformulado e o que não deve ser construído.

The problem comes before the technology

Real problems do not arrive with a model, framework, or algorithm already chosen. They arrive as a problem, an operational context, and available data. Technology is selected in response to the problem — not the other way around.

Experimentation is not the end. The goal is to produce enough evidence to decide what is worth developing, what must be reframed, and what should not be built.
